AeroStudios General-Photo Downloader v0.7.3 — RESUME FIX

Cause: earlier download versions called verify_downloads() on an unfinished
source ledger. That verifier demanded three creator groups even when the
downloader was supposed to fetch more creators.

Fix: during resume, all existing files, paths, hashes, and licence metadata are
still verified, but no three-creator threshold is imposed until dataset
verification/import. The import stage still requires 30 approved photos and
at least three distinct creator groups.

To update: copy this repository's source files over your existing source
without deleting the local training/.venv, dist, release-out, or
E:\AeroPrivate directories.

From the repository root, run:

powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass `
  -File .\training\Build-General-Dataset.ps1 `
  -Phase Download `
  -Workspace "E:\AeroPrivate" `
  -TargetImages 500 `
  -Contact "YOUR_REAL_EMAIL@example.com" `
  -LicenseMode CC0 `
  -Discovery Auto `
  -MaxPerCreator 30

Replace the email with your real contact address. Do not discard the
existing source-ledger.jsonl or SOURCE_LICENSE_REVIEW.csv. Review licence
and image quality before running Import. Output includes diagnostics that
can explain a low yield even after this fix.

Offline tests: python -m unittest discover -s tests -v
Result: 21 passed, including the regression for a two-creator resume.
Not verified: live Wikimedia download or Windows CUDA training.
