# Legal / attribution checklist for AeroStudio public beta

This kit uses the previously shared AeroStudio Rust fork source archive as its basis for launcher scripts, app icons and software notices.

AeroRoom derives from **LightCraft**, AeroFilm from **FilmCraft**, and AeroPhoto from **PhotoCraft**. The relevant upstream source archives contain MIT and Apache-2.0 licence files. The underlying source and modified executables remain subject to their actual component licences. Check upstream project notice/asset documents before publishing.

The packaging tool includes copies of each application's upstream `LICENSE-MIT`, `LICENSE-APACHE`, and the `NOTICE`/`ATTRIBUTION` files that were present in the provided AeroStudio source snapshot. If your compiled builds include other third-party material (fonts, icons, models, codecs, sample images), verify their separate licences and add required notices to the release.

**Branding is separate from code rights.** Do not distribute original ArtCraft trademarks/logos or imply endorsement by their creators. AeroStudio's aviation branding should be distinctive.

The AeroStudio website uses a photograph supplied during this chat, described as a user's AeroRoom export of an F-35A. It is included as a demonstration asset. The person publishing the site must confirm they hold rights to publish the photo, or replace it.

Before public distribution: validate (1) notice obligations, (2) app title and logos everywhere in the binaries, (3) font and icon redistribution rights, (4) all model and media licences, (5) export correctness, and (6) whether source distribution or a source link is appropriate for your chosen licensing approach.

This is a practical release checklist, not a substitute for a legal review.
